This location was known as the mutation point.

Thus, the degree of separation for anomalous substances also became an important criterion to judge whether a cultivation art was good or bad.

In addition, all the cultivation arts that could provide high degrees of separation were controlled by huge forces or powerful clans. These were their important resources. For this point, things were the same regardless of whether the gods arrived in this world or not.

Since people would practice various cultivation arts, the degree of separation for anomalous substances would be different as well. Naturally, the locations of their mutation points would differ as well.
